## Progression Access Summary

| Unlock condition | Towns accessible |
|---|---|
| Game start | Oldale, Petalburg, Rustboro |
| After Devon Goods delivery (post-Badge 1) | Slateport |
| After HM01 Cut / Route 117 | Verdanturf |
| Story progression (pre-Badge 3) | Mauville |
| After Mt. Chimney storyline (pre-Badge 4) | Fallarbor, Lavaridge |
| After Badge 5 (Norman) + HM03 Surf | Fortree, Lilycove |
| After Badge 6 (Winona) + Surf | Mossdeep |
| After Badge 7 (Tate & Liza) + HM08 Dive | Sootopolis |
| After Badge 8 (Juan) + HM07 Waterfall | Ever Grande |

**Currently accessible (4 badges, Fallarbor):** Oldale, Petalburg, Rustboro, Slateport, Verdanturf, Mauville, Fallarbor, Lavaridge
**Next unlock:** Fortree + Lilycove after beating Norman (Badge 5) and getting Surf
